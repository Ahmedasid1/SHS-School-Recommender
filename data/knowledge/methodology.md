# SHS School Recommender: Methodology and Limits

## How the BECE aggregate is computed

The aggregate is the sum of the grades in four core subjects (English, Mathematics, Integrated Science and Social Studies) plus the candidate's best two elective grades. Each grade is a whole number from 1 (best) to 9 (weakest). A lower aggregate is better. The best possible aggregate is 6 and the weakest is 54. The aggregate is always calculated by code, never estimated by the language model.

## Category estimate (unofficial)

Senior High Schools in the GES list are grouped into Categories A, B and C. This project estimates which categories a candidate may consider using unofficial aggregate cutoffs: an aggregate of 10 or lower estimates categories A, B and C; an aggregate of 11 to 20 estimates categories B and C; an aggregate of 21 to 54 estimates category C. These cutoffs are a project heuristic. They are not official CSSPS eligibility bands, and no official source for them has been verified.

## Placement is decided by CSSPS

Actual placement is made by the Computerised School Selection and Placement System (CSSPS). It depends on the candidate's school choices, performance, and the spaces available in each school. This tool does not predict placement and cannot guarantee admission to any school.

## School data

The school list contains 584 schools taken from the 2019 Ghana Education Service (GES) list of public Senior High Schools. For each school it records the category, region, gender (boys, girls or mixed), residency (day, boarding or both) and the programmes offered. The programmes are agric, business, technical, home economics, visual arts, general arts and science. The file does not record when it was obtained, and it has not been checked against the original document school by school. Cutoff aggregates for individual schools are not part of the data.

## When human review is needed

Candidates, parents and counsellors should confirm school details and the placement process with GES or the official CSSPS portal before making decisions. The recommendations are a starting list to explore, not advice to rely on alone.
